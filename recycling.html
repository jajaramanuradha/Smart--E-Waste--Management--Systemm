Recycling Guide

Follow responsible practices when handling electronic waste.

📱

Mobile Phones

Back up data, factory reset the phone and send unusable phones to an authorized recycler.

💻

Laptops

Remove personal data and consider repair, donation or certified recycling.

🔋

Batteries

Do not burn or throw batteries into regular household waste. Use authorized collection points.

🖨️

Printers

Separate cartridges where possible and use authorized e-waste recycling channels.

Safety: Avoid opening damaged batteries or electronic components yourself. Use authorized e-waste recycling facilities.
